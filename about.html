About INSIGHTRA

INSIGHTRA is a modern, frontend-only Data Analytics Dashboard designed to provide quick, secure, and beautiful insights into your data.

Privacy First

All data processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any backend servers.

Modern Design

Experience a stunning glassmorphism UI with a dark theme and vibrant blue highlights.

Instant Visuals

Simply drag and drop your Excel files to instantly generate beautiful charts and summary metrics.
