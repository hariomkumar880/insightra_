About INSIGHTRA

INSIGHTRA is a modern AI-powered Data Analytics Dashboard designed to provide quick, secure, and visually appealing insights from uploaded Excel, CSV, and PDF files.

Privacy First

All file processing happens securely inside the browser without storing user data permanently.

Modern Glassmorphism UI

Experience a premium dark-themed dashboard with smooth animations and neon blue highlights.

Instant Analytics

Upload Excel files to instantly generate: Bar Charts, Pie Charts, Profit/Loss Reports, Max & Min insights.

Smart Chatbot

Built-in AI chatbot instantly answers questions related to uploaded business data and trends.
